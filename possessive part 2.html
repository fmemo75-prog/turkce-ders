Possessive Adjectives

Suffixes that show belonging · Ekler ile sahiplik ifadesi

What are possessive suffixes?

1

In Turkish, possession is shown by adding a suffix to the noun, not by a separate word like "my" or "your".

2

The possessive pronoun (benim, senin…) is optional — the suffix alone conveys the meaning.

3

Vowel harmony changes the suffix vowel: evim (my house) vs arabam (my car).

4

Nouns ending in a vowel take a buffer -s-: araba → arabası (his/her car).

Vowel harmony quick guide

e, i → suffix uses -i · evim
o, u → suffix uses -u · okulum
ö, ü → suffix uses -ü · gözüm
