Section 1

What is the Geniş Zaman?

The aorist tense expresses habitual, repeated, or generally true actions — similar to the English Simple Present.

🇬🇧 When do we use it?
  • Habits & routines: Her sabah kahve içerim. → I drink coffee every morning.
  • General truths: Güneş doğudan doğar. → The sun rises in the east.
  • Likes & preferences: Müzik dinlerim. → I like listening to music.
  • Scheduled future: Yarın gelirim. → I'll come tomorrow (it's planned).
  • Proverbs & facts: Damlaya damlaya göl olur. → Drop by drop a lake forms.
💡 Key difference from Şimdiki Zaman (-iyor)

Şimdiki Zaman = right now, at this moment → Kitap okuyorum = I am reading (right now).
Geniş Zaman = in general, always, habitually → Kitap okurum = I read (in general / as a habit).

Section 2

Positive Form Olumlu

The positive aorist uses either -ir/-ır/-ur/-ür (for consonant-ending verbs) or -r (for vowel-ending verbs), followed by personal endings.

Formula
Consonant-final stem + -ir / -ır / -ur / -ür + personal suffix
Vowel-final stem   + -r + personal suffix
⚠ One-syllable consonant-final verbs are mostly irregular (see Section 5).

Section 3

Negative Form Olumsuz

The negative aorist uses -maz/-mez for all verbs — no vowel harmony variation on the main suffix, but the personal endings still harmonise.

Negative Formula
Verb stem + -maz / -mez + personal suffix
Use -maz after back vowels (a, ı, o, u) · Use -mez after front vowels (e, i, ö, ü)
⚠ Special case: Ben (1st singular)

The 1st person singular negative is -mam / -mem, NOT -mazım/mezim.
Yazmam (I don't write) — İçmem (I don't drink) — Gelmem (I don't come)

Section 4

Question Form Soru

Add the question particle mi/mı/mu/mü after the aorist suffix, written separately. Personal endings attach to the question particle.

Question Formula
Aorist form (without pers. suffix) + mi / mı / mu / mü + personal suffix
The question particle is always written as a separate word.
yazmak — question
Benyazar mıyım?Do I write?
Senyazar mısın?Do you write?
Oyazar mı?Does he/she write?
Bizyazar mıyız?Do we write?
Sizyazar mısınız?Do you write?
Onlaryazarlar mı?Do they write?
💡 Negative question

Combine negative + question: Yazmaz mısın? → Don't you write? / Won't you write?
This is also used for polite requests: Yazar mısınız? → Would you write? (polite)

Section 5

Irregular Verbs — Düzensiz Fiiller

Most one-syllable verbs ending in a consonant are irregular in the positive aorist — they use -ar/-er instead of the expected -ır/-ir. These must be memorised!

⚡ The Big Rule

Multi-syllable consonant-final verbs → regular -ir/-ır/-ur/-ür
Single-syllable consonant-final verbs → mostly irregular, use -ar/-er
