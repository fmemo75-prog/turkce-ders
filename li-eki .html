-lı / -li / -lu / -lü
The "with / having" Suffix

One suffix, four vowel forms, many meanings — with annotated examples, TTS audio, and type-in exercises.

Four meanings of the same suffix

🎁
Meaning 1 · With / Having

"Equipped with"

Added to a noun → adjective meaning "having X" or "with X". The most common use.

şekerli çay
tea with sugar
📍
Meaning 2 · From / Of a place

"From that place"

Added to a city or region → shows origin, like a demonym. Very common in introductions.

İstanbullu
person from Istanbul
😊
Meaning 3 · Characterised by

"Full of / marked by"

Describes a quality or emotional state — "having the quality of X".

umutlu
hopeful (full of hope)
🔢
Meaning 4 · Numbered / Labelled

"Bearing that number"

Added to numbers or codes to describe something labelled or identified by that number.

100lük bir not
a score of 100

Vowel harmony

Which form to use? Follow the last vowel of the root.

a, ı → use -lı  · çay → çaylı
e, i → use -li  · süt → sütli… wait: ü → -lü!
o, u → use -lu  · tuz → tuzlu
ö, ü → use -lü  · göz → gözlü

Nouns ending in a vowel: just attach directly — araba → arabalı. No buffer consonant needed.
